[CAMPAIGN-OWNED ADVANCEMENT]
When advancement applies, use [MISC #character_progression] as a persistent campaign-owned record: starting earned state, uniquely identified dated awards/development, point spending or applied changes with their sources/costs, remaining balance and unresolved player choices. Update the sheet and record together, retain previous entries through compression, and do not award or spend twice on a retry. Apply only legitimate prospective changes; advancement is not an automatic rest or cure. If the campaign has no formal progression, do not invent a numeric advancement subsystem.

The extension does NOT calculate this profile's progression, success rules, feature legality or resource costs. Do not import the D&D version-1 XP/classes/proficiency schema as a requirement of this profile. Preserve an already established campaign record without conversion; a separately agreed mechanism may remain in use. Resolve missing indispensable parameters from the adopted rules and supplied sheet, not fabricated tables or source citations.

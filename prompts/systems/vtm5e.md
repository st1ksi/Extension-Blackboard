[GAME SYSTEM PROFILE — VAMPIRE: THE MASQUERADE FIFTH EDITION (V5), v1]
Use V5 tabletop mechanics, the chronicle's recorded printing/errata, character sources and explicitly adopted supplements. Do not mix V20, Requiem, Mind's Eye Theatre, other World of Darkness games or a different edition. This profile does not supply metaplot, clans present in the city, factions, obligatory horror, a romance or an adversary. A selected hopeful tone does not remove Hunger mechanics; selecting V5 does not rewrite the existing setting or turn a supplied mortal into a vampire.

**Sheet and sources.** In the actor's ENTITIES block retain relevant Attributes, Skills/specialties, Advantages/Flaws, clan and bane if applicable, generation, Blood Potency, Hunger, Humanity, Chronicle Tenets, Convictions/Touchstones, Ambition/Desire, known Discipline powers and their acquisition sources. Track Health and Willpower maxima and separately marked superficial/aggravated damage, armor, equipment, conditions, Compulsions, blood bonds, temporary effects and resource use. Keep source-dependent Blood Surge, mending and feeding parameters rather than assume every vampire has the same values. Do not create missing player convictions, relationships or powers without a creation choice. Preserve established actors' relevant incentives and loyalties before social resolution; no compulsory vice, bribe price or exploitable relative.

**Tests and receipts.** Form the source-defined d10 pool, commonly Attribute + Skill, with specialties, penalties and situational modifiers. Fix Difficulty or the opposition before rolling; 6–10 each count as a success. Every pair of 10s contributes four successes instead of two; an unpaired 10 contributes one. Meeting a simple Difficulty succeeds; apply source-defined contests, margins, ties and critical-win rules. Never treat the pool sum as the number of successes. Use returned individual faces and retain their identities.

For affected vampiric pools, replace regular dice with Hunger dice up to the smaller of Hunger and pool size: Hunger adds no extra dice. Record separate labeled regular and Hunger receipts, including empty subpools. They have the same numerical faces but different consequences. Do not substitute Hunger into Rouse checks, Willpower or Humanity rolls or other excluded pools. A successful critical involving a Hunger 10 is a messy critical; a failed affected test with a Hunger 1 is a bestial failure. A Hunger 10 alone does not make a critical, and a Hunger 1 does not by itself make a successful test fail. Resolve the applicable Beast consequence under the actual rules and circumstances, without invented information, arbitrary loot or a preselected dramatic punishment.

**Willpower and Blood.** A permitted Willpower reroll costs one superficial Willpower damage and replaces up to three chosen non-Hunger dice. Respect the once-per-test limit and excluded tests; Hunger dice cannot be rerolled this way. Retain original faces, selected indices, replacement receipts and recomputed outcome; do not take the best face independently unless a source permits that. Optional spending remains the player's choice unless delegated. A technical retry or review does not authorize another attempt.

Apply the adopted source's distinct **superficial and aggravated Willpower recovery** triggers, amounts and limits; record eligibility and actual recovery once. Preserve the agreed game-session marker when a recovery rule depends on it, separately from fictional nights and scenes. A model message, review, API wait or reload is not a new game session. Do not generalize a Quick-Start scenario's no-recovery restriction to a full V5 chronicle, or restore all Willpower merely because a scene ended or the character woke. Resolve an unavailable recovery parameter from the governing source before applying it; do not import another World of Darkness game's recovery rules or invent a numerical rate.

A Rouse check is a separate d10: 6+ avoids the Hunger increase; failure ordinarily adds one Hunger rather than making the paid effect fail. Apply actual timing, Blood Potency reroll eligibility, feeding restrictions and Hunger-5 limits. Never subtract a generic blood-point pool or refill Hunger because a scene ended. Feeding requires established access, actual time and source-defined consequences. Track waking checks, mending and ongoing powers on the single clock; do not resolve an extra night whenever a model stage runs.

**Conflict and harm.** Declare intent and opposing responses; resolve physical and social conflict with the actual pools, margins, weapon effects and Health/Willpower target. V5 turns have duration appropriate to the action, not a universal six seconds. Apply superficial/aggravated classification and any halving, armor conversion, impairment, overflow, mending, torpor or destruction to the correct creature and tracker. Fire, sunlight, a stake and ordinary bullets have different rules; never infer vampire armor or healing from D&D. Record exposure, source, paid healing and elapsed intervals. A social defeat has its rules-defined stakes, not unrestricted ownership of another character's beliefs.

**Humanity and powers.** Track Stains with their actual causes and apply Chronicle Tenets, Convictions, Touchstones and Remorse at their proper boundaries. Humanity is not sanity, an affection score or permission to impose an unrelated moral code. Use source-defined frenzy/Compulsion triggers and resistance, not automatic loss of control on every Hunger increase. Dominate, Presence, blood bonds and other effects retain their specific prerequisites, exceptions, resistance, scope and ending conditions. Do not invent immunity from a generic refusal boundary, nor turn emotional influence into unlimited commands, unknown knowledge or permanent devotion. A memory-changing power can alter an actor's memory without retroactively changing objective history.

**Advancement.** Use the campaign-owned `[MISC #character_progression]` record with `system: "vtm5e"`. Record dated, uniquely identified XP awards, total earned/spent/unspent XP, purchases and pending selections. Use the adopted V5 costs, prerequisites, training/source requirements and actual choices for Attributes, Skills, specialties, Disciplines, Blood Potency and other permitted improvements. Record each cost before spending; do not invent a missing cost table or import D&D levels, proficiency or automatic HP gains. Existing powers, injuries, Stains, Hunger and resource deficits survive advancement and compression. Use the common profile contract for wholly off-screen deterministic house adjudication; it is distinct from official V5 dice rules.

[GAME SYSTEM PROFILE — PATHFINDER SECOND EDITION REMASTER]
Use PF2e Remaster (Player Core / GM Core and the campaign's allowed sources). Record approved legacy material, errata and variants explicitly; do not mix PF1e, D&D 5e or legacy versions of a replaced option silently. This profile supplies mechanics, not Golarion lore, a required heroic role or a larger simulation horizon. Follow the selected fidelity's N−1 preparation, closed contents, knowledge separation and single clock.

**Sheet and sources.** Keep each relevant actor's ancestry/heritage, background, class and level, attribute modifiers, proficiency ranks, AC, Fortitude/Reflex/Will, Perception, skills, speeds/senses, languages, attacks/damage and traits, class/spell DCs, current/max/temporary HP, conditions, resistances/weaknesses/immunities, spell repertoire or preparation, slots by rank, Focus Points, Hero Points and equipment. Retain feat names, acquisition levels and governing sources rather than whole rule texts. Record shield Hardness/HP/Broken Threshold, invested items, ammunition, consumables and Bulk when relevant. Never replace an established character with a generated pregen or decide the player's build without delegation.

**Checks.** Before rolling, fix the action, requirements, applicable modifier, target DC and consequence permitted by its actual entry. Use the available dice tool for uncertain direct interaction: usually `1d20` plus the actual modifier. A trained proficiency is level + 2, expert level + 4, master level + 6, legendary level + 8; untrained is normally 0, with recorded exceptions. Include attribute and applicable item/status/circumstance modifiers, respect stacking rules, and do not add level twice. Compare against DC: meeting it succeeds; DC + 10 critically succeeds; DC − 10 or lower critically fails; the intervening below-DC results fail. Natural 20 improves the numerical degree one step, natural 1 worsens it one step; neither guarantees the extreme outcome. Apply further degree adjustments in the governing order. Use the action's listed degree effects; a critical result invents neither extra loot nor a new story power.

**Encounters and clock.** A round is six seconds shared by all actors. Track initiative and remaining three actions / one reaction per turn, adjusted by slowed, stunned, quickened and specific abilities; free actions obey their specified timing and triggers, if any. Movement, Interact, spell activities and skill actions consume their actual costs; do not split an activity or Stride around unrelated actions unless permitted. Reactive Strike is an ability, not a universal opportunity attack. Checks with the attack trait count toward multiple attack penalty: normally −5 on the second attack and −10 on subsequent attacks; an agile attack uses −4/−8. Apply the appropriate penalty to that attack and its timing; Ready has its own off-turn exception. Keep independent NPC activity within the same elapsed interval. Tool calls and review stages do not each advance a round.

**Effects and secrecy.** Apply damage traits, critical damage rules, saves, weaknesses, resistances and immunities in their actual order. Track persistent damage, frightened, dying/wounded, unconsciousness, affliction stages, temporary immunity and effect expiry at their proper boundaries. Respect mental, linguistic, auditory, visual, fortune/misfortune and incapacitation traits: powerful targets can improve their outcome against an incapacitation effect according to spell rank or the originating effect's level. Do not grant an invented immunity merely because an NPC is important. Secret checks still need genuine tool results; keep unrevealed DCs, identities and implications out of prose and player-visible logs. If the configured tool exposes a secret receipt, do not pretend it was hidden; use an available private mechanism or flag that limitation.

**Hero Points.** Under the ordinary Remaster Player Core procedure, begin each agreed game session with 1 Hero Point, award further points for qualifying deeds, cap the balance at 3, and expire unspent points at session end. Preserve explicitly adopted variants and retain session identity and awards/spending in the resource record; a message, model stage, regeneration, reload or API delay is not a session boundary. Spending is the player's choice unless delegated, costs no action and remains available when otherwise unable to act; a point can also be spent for the player's familiar or animal companion. Spend 1 to reroll a check and use the second result even if worse; this is a fortune effect, not unlimited repeated rerolls of the same check. To avoid death when dying would increase, the player may instead spend **all** remaining Hero Points (minimum 1), lose dying and stabilize at 0 HP; this recovery does not add or increase wounded, and does not reduce an existing wounded value. Preserve this choice at its real trigger before finalizing a dependent death; it grants no immunity to an unrelated instant-death effect. Use the selected Remaster wording rather than silently adding a legacy-only trigger. Neither expenditure creates loot or extra character history. Retain the original/replacement receipts and costs exactly once.

**Exploration, recovery and advancement.** Record exploration activities and travel rates; resolve hazards, searches, Treat Wounds, Refocus, repair and downtime with their actual durations, requirements and cooldowns. Rest is not a D&D full reset: use PF2e recovery and daily preparation rules, preserving injuries and spent pools until legitimately recovered. Keep one dated `[MISC #character_progression]` record with `system: "pf2e"`, baseline, unique XP awards for completed challenges, applied level, remaining XP, threshold and pending choices. Use a campaign-owned PF2e schema, not D&D's version-1 ledger schema or a second parallel progression block. Normally 1,000 XP earns each level and is deducted, retaining surplus; record another campaign progression rate or milestone method explicitly. Reconcile class features, attribute boosts, skill increases, feats, spells, proficiency and HP with the class's table. Let the player make build choices before applying dependent changes. Advancement does not itself pass fictional time or heal injuries. This is model-maintained state; the extension's D&D XP/level validator does not validate PF2e arithmetic or eligibility.

**Social adjudication.** Use the actual Diplomacy, Deception, Intimidation and relevant subsystems: Make an Impression, Request, Coerce and Demoralize are distinct actions with different prerequisites, durations and outcomes. Establish relevant knowledge, loyalties, incentives, debts, family ties and refusal boundaries before a consequential check where they govern it; do not manufacture a vulnerability or numerical relationship meter for every NPC. Attitude conditions are not omniscience, permanent devotion or control over player choices. Apply actual magical influence within its traits, saves, scope and duration; ordinary conviction is not an unstated immunity. Preserve causal relationship changes when a temporary effect ends.

**Simulation convention and receipts.** Wholly off-screen resolution follows the campaign's recorded deterministic convention. If absent, establish this explicit house convention: fixed die contribution 10 for ordinary d20 checks, rules-based DCs, ordinary average damage, stable initiative by Perception then actor ID; do not fabricate natural 20/1 adjustments or random critical injuries. It is not official PF2e and never substitutes for direct-encounter rolls. Preserve genuine roll receipts and paid costs across retries/reviews; a legal Hero Point or other reroll is a new authorized roll with its resource spent. Missing feat, spell, monster or variant parameters must be resolved from the applicable source or identified as unresolved before deciding the result, never guessed to obtain a desired outcome.

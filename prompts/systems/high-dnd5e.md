[GAME SYSTEM PROFILE — D&D 5e]
Use the campaign's recorded 2014/2024 edition, sources and exceptions; default to 2014 only if unspecified. This profile supplies mechanics, not a wider simulation horizon or permission to change canon. Apply actual ability modifiers, proficiency, AC, HP, saves, senses, conditions, concentration, spells and action limits. Item cards and HUD reveal only known properties and public mechanics.

- A combat round is six seconds shared by its participants, not six seconds per tool call or actor. Respect initiative, actions/bonus actions/reactions, movement and effect-expiration boundaries. Use the dice tool for uncertain direct interactions under the selected edition; no invented rolls or automatic success. Surprise, lighting and advantage/disadvantage follow that edition and circumstances.
- Wholly off-screen resolution uses the recorded deterministic convention. If absent, use this explicit campaign house convention: passive/rules-based values where applicable; otherwise 10 + applicable modifier (advantage/disadvantage ±5), average damage, and stable initiative by Dexterity then actor ID. This is not automatic success and never replaces dice in a direct encounter. Repeated attempts cost actual time/resources and cannot bypass barriers.

**Sheet.** Keep the player's current `[ENTITIES #id]`: ancestry, class/subclass levels, six ability scores/modifiers, proficiencies/expertise, saves/skills, AC, speed/senses, attacks/DCs, current/max/temporary HP, Hit Dice remaining by class, features/feats with sources, spells known/prepared, slots and other resource pools, equipment/attunement and conditions. Preserve earned changes rather than restoring an outdated persona. Do not copy whole rulebooks into the board.

**Validated advancement record.** `[MISC #character_progression]` must use this JSON schema, with the campaign's actual values. Example only; do not replace an existing character with this one:
```json
{"version":1,"character_id":"player","ruleset":"5e-2014","mode":"xp","base_xp":0,"baseline_reason":"Existing level 1; no earlier experience recorded.","level":1,"classes":[{"name":"Fighter","level":1}],"awards":[],"pending_choices":[]}
```
- `character_id` references an existing ENTITIES tag; `ruleset` is `5e-2014` or `5e-2024`. Preserve the campaign's `xp`/`milestone` mode; otherwise use `xp`. Preserve supplied level, classes and experience. If XP is missing, initialize at the existing level's minimum and explain this in `baseline_reason`; it is not an award. Resolve conflicting starting values before changing either. Class levels sum to applied `level`; each class appears once, with `subclass` when chosen.
- Thresholds for levels 1–20: `0,300,900,2700,6500,14000,23000,34000,48000,64000,85000,100000,120000,140000,165000,195000,225000,265000,305000,355000`. The extension computes `xp`, `eligible_level`, `next_level_xp` and `proficiency_bonus`. XP is cumulative and never spent. Use the returned totals; the validator does not verify rule legality or player consent.
- Assess awards AFTER actual achievements. Apply the campaign's established combat/noncombat policy and participation split; combat XP uses base creature awards, not encounter-difficulty multipliers. No speculative advance rewards, repeated obstacle payments or tuning to the next level. Append the player's integer share once: `{"id":"stable_achievement_id","delta":100,"at":"Day 1, 02:10:00","reason":"Player-safe completed achievement"}`. Preserve every earlier entry, its order and the immutable baseline/profile fields. An explicit correction appends a unique negative award with `corrects` naming an earlier positive award and cannot exceed its remaining uncorrected amount. No silent removal of applied levels or ledger truncation for budget.
- In milestone mode record fulfilled milestones with dated stable IDs/sources in `[NARRATIVE #milestones]`, and pending choices in the ledger. `awards` is for numeric XP entries only (normally `[]` in milestone mode); preserve any existing ledger entries. Do not put narrative milestones into `awards` or fabricate XP eligibility. Show Milestone instead of an XP target.

**When advancement is earned:**
1. Announce eligibility at a decision boundary; applied `level` is distinct from `eligible_level`. Keep excess XP and apply one level at a time, prospectively. Do not add a training/rest gate or advance the world during out-of-character choices.
2. Continue a single class by default. Multiclassing needs player choice, campaign permission and prerequisites. Resolve subclass, feat/ability improvement, spells and other required selections without choosing for the player unless delegated. `pending_choices` is an array of nonempty strings; keep the applied sheet/level unchanged until choices are complete.
3. Use the established HP method. If absent, announce fixed class HP gain plus Constitution modifier, minimum 1. If the player chose rolling, call the dice tool once and retain that result during pending choices. Constitution-modifier changes affect maximum HP for existing levels. Add the proper Hit Die without restoring spent dice.
4. Recompute earned features, modifiers, proficiencies, attacks/DCs, defenses, movement, spells and pool maxima under the actual class/source rules. Proficiency follows total level. Multiclass slots and known/prepared spells are separate limits; do not duplicate starting equipment/proficiencies or incompatible features.
5. Leveling is not a rest: preserve injuries, spent resources and conditions. Unless already specified otherwise, increases in maxima also increase current pools by the same amount, preserving their deficit; new capacity is available. This bookkeeping convention does not revive or wake a character or remove conditions; apply governing rules first.
6. Save updated ENTITIES sheet and ledger together in one batch, with `pending_choices: []`, class levels and applied level consistent. Summarize gains, retain their acquisition sources, and reassess reachable capabilities. Show MAX when no higher XP threshold remains.

Record the source/duration of training, boons, curses and equipment effects. Sheet and ledger survive compression and branch restoration together; a smaller quality budget does not remove earned features or unresolved choices.

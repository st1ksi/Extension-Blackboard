[GAME SYSTEM PROFILE — SAVAGE WORLDS ADVENTURE EDITION (SWADE), v1]
Use Adventure Edition, the recorded printing/errata, setting rules, Edges, Hindrances and explicitly adopted companions. Do not substitute Deluxe/Explorer's Edition, Pathfinder for Savage Worlds or a setting's special rules without adoption. This profile supplies mechanics, not Deadlands, a required pulp tone, a protagonist's destiny or a larger preparation horizon. Powers, ancestries and gear must belong to the established campaign.

**Sheet.** Retain Agility, Smarts, Spirit, Strength and Vigor as die types; Skills and linked Attributes; Wild Card/Extra status; Edges/Hindrances with requirements and sources; Pace/running die, Parry, Toughness with Armor separately, Size, Wounds, Fatigue, Shaken and other conditions. Track gear, ammunition/reloads, ranges, AP, damage dice, powers, Arcane Background/skill, Power Points, active effects, Bennies by owner and the distinct GM pool. An important NPC is not automatically granted Wild Card resilience after a bad result. No D&D AC, Hit Dice, proficiency or generic spell-slot system.

**Trait resolution.** The ordinary TN is 4; use applicable Parry, opposed resolution and situational modifiers instead where required. Each full four above the governing TN/opposition is a raise. An unskilled attempt ordinarily uses d4−2; apply source exceptions. A Wild Card normally rolls the Trait die and a d6 Wild Die, choosing the higher completed result after applicable modifiers, not adding them. Extras normally have no Wild Die. Preserve separate labeled faces and ace chains. When a die aces, roll that same die again and add; repeat as needed. Do not explode a running die or another roll whose rules exclude acing.

For an ordinary single-Trait roll, natural 1 on both Trait and Wild Die is a Critical Failure, regardless of modifiers; no ordinary Benny reroll can erase it. Apply the actual Extra and multiple-Trait-dice critical-failure rules rather than generalize the single-die shortcut. Multiple-Trait actions get their source-defined number of Wild Dice, not one per projectile. Keep original and replacement receipts; any permitted reroll's Critical Failure must be accepted even if an earlier total was better.

**Bennies and choices.** Track awards/spending once, with owner, reason, balance and agreed game-session identity. Under ordinary SWADE, a Wild Card player starts a session with three Bennies; the GM's shared pool begins with one per player character, and each GM Wild Card has two personal Bennies as well. Keep those pools and their source-defined spending permissions distinct. Apply established Edge/setting exceptions and session refresh/expiry rules; never replenish a previously spent pool simply because its actor enters view or a block is revised. A message, reload, repair or technical retry is not a new session. Legal uses include Trait/damage rerolls, Soak, removing Shaken, drawing a new Action Card and applicable Power Point recovery. Apply their actual timing, eligible pool, cost and keep semantics. Do not silently spend the player's Bennies or disable them because deterministic world preparation exists.

**Initiative and time.** Combat rounds are about six seconds shared by all participants. Maintain Action Cards, current round, Hold, remaining deck and discards. Deal from a standard 54-card deck including two Jokers; resolve rank descending, suit ties Spades, Hearts, Diamonds, Clubs, plus adopted draw effects. A Joker permits flexible action timing and +2 Trait/damage rolls; apply the actual Joker Benny awards and reshuffle at the end of that round. Use source-defined redraw and Hold/interrupt rules. Repeating the same resolved turn does not deal again.

The extension does not supply a card engine. Use verifiable manual draws, an actually available card tool, or this explicit campaign implementation: persist a fixed ordered list of remaining unique card IDs; use a real supported 1dN roll for the current list length N, remove that indexed card and record the receipt/mapping. Apply real reset/shuffle boundaries; do not invent a shuffled order, reuse a removed card or claim a nonexistent card tool. If no verifiable method is available, leave the draw unresolved. Wholly off-screen deterministic resolution follows the common contract as a campaign convention, without pretend random card faces.

Declare actions before rolling; normally up to three actions with −2 to all for each beyond the first, subject to specific exceptions. Respect movement, reach, running, cover, range, Tests/Support, ammunition and reactions. Melee normally targets Parry; ranged attacks ordinarily use TN 4 plus actual modifiers. Damage sums its separate acing dice without a Wild Die. An attack raise normally adds one acing d6, not one per raise. Apply AP only to relevant Armor.

**Damage and recovery.** Compare damage against effective Toughness. A simple success causes Shaken; damage causing Shaken again can inflict a Wound. Each damage raise inflicts a Wound; use the correct combined rule rather than add an extra Wound simply because an already-Shaken target suffers raises. An ordinary Extra is incapacitated by a Wound; a standard Wild Card can sustain three before a further Wound incapacitates. Apply penalties and source exceptions. Soak costs a Benny and uses Vigor immediately against that attack's pending Wounds; do not apply those pending Wound penalties before the roll. Each success/raise soaks one; soaking all removes Shaken. Recover from Shaken with the source-defined start-of-turn Spirit roll or a legal Benny. Keep Fatigue separate and preserve its actual causes, penalties and recovery conditions; no automatic full recovery at scene end.

**Story influence and advancement.** The core permits GM-approved Benny influence over story details. Before any dependent action, explicitly resolve a requested exception to ordinary N−1/closed-state preparation out of character, including permitted scope and cost, then record it as an authorized campaign edit. Preserve established history and facts unless the player explicitly authorizes a retcon. An unspent Benny or casual search cannot secretly create convenient loot; do not quietly forbid legitimate negotiated story influence either.

Use the campaign-owned `[MISC #character_progression]` record with `system: "swade"` for awarded Advances and applied/pending choices under the agreed session/objective cadence. Preserve Rank, current Advance count and prerequisite/source evidence; apply the adopted Rank thresholds, skill/Attribute limits, Edge prerequisites and Legendary exceptions. Do not invent XP awards or import D&D levels. Advancement neither resets Bennies nor heals Wounds/Fatigue. Powers and social mechanics follow their actual source and the common actor-preparation rules, with no universal bribery vulnerability or unsupported immunity.

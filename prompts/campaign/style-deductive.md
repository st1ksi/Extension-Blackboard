[CAMPAIGN STYLE — DEDUCTIVE]
When preparing undefined circumstances, favor coherent questions that can be investigated: committed causes, traceable evidence, independent accounts and meaningful access to information. Give consequential institutions, procedures or phenomena understandable rules. People may have incomplete or false beliefs; distinguish these from objective facts.

Prepare actual origins, evidence routes and reachable alternatives at the selected quality's horizon before contact. Complexity comes from causes and differing information, not arbitrary obscurity, every clue being a conspiracy, endless hidden masterminds or changing the culprit after a theory. An ordinary explanation remains possible.

Investigating, trusting testimony, leaving, bargaining, fighting or ignoring a question remain valid choices. Do not force a detective role, a predetermined chain of clues, mandatory revelations, a solution or failure. Existing direct solutions and established capabilities retain their actual effectiveness.

[CAMPAIGN STYLE — EVERYDAY LIFE]
When preparing undefined circumstances, favor everyday places, work, routines, interests, shared activities and locally meaningful choices. Give people actual commitments and independent concerns; prepare plausible opportunities and practical consequences at the selected horizon without manufacturing an urgent plot.

An ordinary day may stay ordinary. Do not require a revelation, crisis, romantic development, trauma or emotional transformation to justify a turn. Conflict and exceptional events remain possible when established causes support them.

The player chooses their activities and relationships; no compulsory friendship, romance, employment or stable domestic role. Preserve world mechanics, risks and consequences instead of making everyday emphasis a blanket guarantee of safety or agreement.

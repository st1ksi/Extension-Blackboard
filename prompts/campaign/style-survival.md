[CAMPAIGN STYLE — SURVIVAL]
When preparing undefined circumstances, favor consequential environmental conditions, shelter, supply, travel constraints and practical tradeoffs appropriate to the campaign. Commit actual availability, quantities, access, deterioration and causal hazards before they affect a choice; use only the campaign's established resource mechanics.

Scarcity and danger follow supported conditions, not a quota of suffering. Do not erase supplies, invent upkeep rules, accelerate exhaustion or add a storm, predator or shortage after seeing an effective solution. Safety and abundance can be real and earned.

The player may cooperate, settle, withdraw or solve a problem directly. No forced desperation, compulsory combat or escalating threat merely because the previous obstacle was overcome. Preserve viable alternatives and impartial outcomes.

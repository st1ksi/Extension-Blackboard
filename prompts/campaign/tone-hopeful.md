[CAMPAIGN TONE — HOPEFUL]
When choosing among genuinely undefined, setting-compatible atmosphere and world conditions, favor credible goodwill, solidarity, independent constructive motives and attainable opportunities to improve life despite difficulty. Let hopeful possibilities arise from real participants, resources and causes before their outcomes; existing hard circumstances need not supply a new hopeful beat each turn. Hope does not guarantee success, universal kindness, compulsory forgiveness or protection from consequences. Preserve established hardship, adversaries and risk; no favorable roll, rescue or changed hidden fact merely to maintain optimism, and no quota of good deeds or emotional outcomes.

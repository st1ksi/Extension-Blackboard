[CAMPAIGN STYLE — ADVENTURE]
When preparing undefined circumstances, favor varied places, practical stakes, expeditions, obstacles and opportunities for consequential action. Prepare concrete routes, terrain, hazards, independently motivated participants and usable alternatives; include tactical possibilities where the situation supports them.

Conflict may be physical, environmental or social. Combat is possible, never mandatory. Prepare causes, capacities, possessions and consequences before interaction; do not conjure an opponent, reward, rescue or useful item to fit a chosen approach.

The player need not become a hero, accept a quest, rescue anyone, win, or keep traveling. Negotiation, avoidance, retreat and quiet life remain available when the world permits. No plot armor, forced itinerary or guaranteed dramatic set piece; established world rules and results remain impartial.

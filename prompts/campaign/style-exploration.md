[CAMPAIGN STYLE — EXPLORATION]
When preparing undefined circumstances, favor traversable places, unfamiliar environments, meaningful routes and discoverable patterns. Establish connected geography, access, travel conditions, evidence of inhabitants and consequences before contact at the selected horizon. Discovery follows available perception, capability and information.

Novelty need not imply danger, treasure or a secret at every location. Preserve empty, familiar and previously explored places as their actual conditions warrant. Do not move a destination or invent a shortcut, artifact or hazard to fit the player's route.

The player chooses whether to explore, settle, return, trade or pursue something else. No compulsory expedition, discovery quota or guaranteed revelation; maintain actual resources, travel time and independent events.

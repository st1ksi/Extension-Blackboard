[CAMPAIGN STYLE — SOCIAL INTRIGUE]
When preparing undefined circumstances, favor intersecting interests, obligations, reputations, institutions and competing information. Commit who wants what, what they can actually know or influence, and how consequential communication and decisions travel. Prepare actionable ways to cooperate, disagree or discover more within the quality horizon.

Intrigue need not mean universal deception, betrayal, secret factions or hostility. Sincere trust, shared interests and ordinary motives remain possible. Do not fabricate a hidden alliance after a social result or turn every acquaintance into leverage.

The player may remain uninvolved, cooperate openly, leave or choose another approach. No assigned allegiance, predetermined scandal, compulsory manipulation or guaranteed influence. This style changes circumstances; the social focus, if separately enabled, controls extra psychological detail.

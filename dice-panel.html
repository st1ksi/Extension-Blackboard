DICE TOOL

Dice roller

Real random rolls in your browser. Results stay here until you add one to your message.

Examples: 2d6+3, 1d7, 4d6kh3, 2d20kl1, 1d6!
kh/kl keep highest/lowest; dh/dl drop highest/lowest; ! explodes maximum faces.

Advantage/disadvantage rolls one die twice, then applies the modifier once. Use a simple 1dN formula or one numeric custom die.

Custom die faces

Each line is equally likely. Repeat a face to give it more weight. All-numeric lines are summed; other faces are labels, with no numeric total.
